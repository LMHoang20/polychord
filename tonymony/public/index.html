Check my 4-part harmony

Write Soprano and Alto on the treble staff and Tenor and Bass on the bass staff, add a Roman numeral under each chord, and TonyMony marks every broken rule as you go.

How to write
  • Add notes: pick a voice (S A T B) and a duration, then click the staff. Or type the letters A–G.
  • Change a note: click it, then click higher or lower in its column, or use ↑/↓ (Shift = octave), the accidental buttons, - flat, = sharp, n natural, 1–5 duration, . dot, R rest, ⌫ delete. ←/→ walk through the notes, Esc lets go.
  • Chords: click the + under a beat and type (I, ii6, IV64, V65/V, viiø7, N6, It+6, Fr+6, Ger+6, Cad64…). Tab moves to the next beat. Or click the chord buttons above the staff to fill the beats in order. Type o for °, % for ø. The Extensions buttons add a 9, 11 or 13, or an alteration (b5, #5, b9, #9, #11, b13), to the chord at that beat.
  • Switch voice: Alt+1–4. Undo with Ctrl/⌘+Z. Your work is saved in this browser.

Results

    How I read your chords
      Rules checked
      • Errors: parallel 5ths, octaves and unisons; notes that aren't in the chord; missing root, 3rd or 7th; wrong bass note for the inversion; doubled leading tone, 7th, ♭6 or ♯4; voice crossing and overlap; S–A or A–T more than an octave apart, T–B more than two octaves; augmented melodic intervals; cross relations (a chromatic change should stay in one voice).
      • Warnings: contrary 5ths/octaves; direct (hidden) 5ths/octaves in the outer voices; unequal 5ths into the bass; omitted 5th without a doubled root; 6/4 chords without a doubled bass; leaps of a 7th or more than an octave; unresolved 7ths, leading tones and augmented-6th tones; notes outside the usual range; bars that don't add up.
