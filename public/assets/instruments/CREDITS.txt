Instrument recordings

The MP3s in this folder come from the Fluid R3 GM soundfont by Frank Wen,
as pre-rendered by Benjamin Gleitzman in midi-js-soundfonts:
https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts (FluidR3_GM)

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

TonyPoly uses a subset (one note every 3 semitones from C2 to C7), renamed by
MIDI note number. The recordings themselves are unchanged.
