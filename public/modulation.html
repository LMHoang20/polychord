Modulation

Ear-training for key changes — listen to a modulating 8- or 16-chord progression and transcribe it.

Exercise

Generate a progression that moves through 2–3 keys (2 keys = 8 chords, 3 keys = 16 chords), then transcribe each chord below. Press Play to listen (browsers need a click to start audio).

Your Transcription

Type each chord you hear — e.g. C, Am, G7, Bdim. Enharmonics (C# = Db) and quality spellings (m = min) are accepted. Use ▶ on a slot to replay a single chord; press Enter to move to the next.
